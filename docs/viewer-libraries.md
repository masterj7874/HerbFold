# Scientific 3D viewer and animation choices

The new React 19 interface uses five complementary packages from the requested list. Installed versions are locked in `frontend/package-lock.json`: Three.js 0.185.1, React Three Fiber 9.7.0, Drei 10.7.8, GSAP 3.15.0, and Motion 13.2.0. The viewer does not need a second animation engine, smooth-scroll layer, particle system, or decorative shader. Page scrolling remains native.

- **Three.js + React Three Fiber** render actual atom coordinates and bond records. Atoms share one instanced sphere mesh; bond rails share one instanced cylinder mesh. Rendering uses demand mode when the camera is idle, with continuous frames only during opt-in rotation. This follows the official [R3F scaling/performance guidance](https://r3f.docs.pmnd.rs/advanced/scaling-performance) and [Three.js InstancedMesh contract](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/InstancedMesh.html).
- **Drei** supplies OrbitControls, HTML atom labels and the measured-distance line. Use the **mouse wheel**, two-finger touch pinch, or the labelled zoom buttons to zoom. Wheel gestures over the canvas control the camera; the page scrolls normally outside it. Focus the canvas and use **+ / −** to zoom or **0** to fit the whole structure. The displayed percentage is calculated from the actual camera distance relative to a fitted view. Controls are scoped to the viewport and clean up on unmount. See the official [Drei controls documentation](https://drei.docs.pmnd.rs/controls/introduction).
- **GSAP** interpolates the camera position and orbit target together for fit, zoom and atom focus. User gestures cancel the current transition. Every transition is killed on cleanup; camera transforms are not reverted to an older position. GSAP explicitly supports animating [numeric properties of arbitrary objects](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/GSAP/gsap.to()).
- **Motion** animates inspector, settings and provenance panels. The viewer honors `prefers-reduced-motion`, disables automatic rotation for that preference, and makes camera transitions immediate. Motion's role is limited to UI state transitions.

## Data and scientific semantics

`MoleculeViewer` accepts a typed `MolecularScene` from the molecular API. No coordinates or bonds are invented by the renderer. Coordinates and distance measurements are in Å, and reported atom indices refer to the source JSON indices. See `frontend/src/types/molecular.ts` and `docs/molecular-viewer.md` for the data contract.

The four representations are ball-and-stick, sticks, van der Waals space filling, and a Cα backbone trace. Single, double, triple, and aromatic bonds are distinguished; aromatic bonds use one continuous and one dashed rail. The Cα trace connects consecutive residues within one chain and breaks across implausible distances or sequence gaps. It is explicitly described as a coordinate trace, not a secondary-structure ribbon assignment.

Selecting an atom exposes its element, index, atom name, formal charge where available, residue/chain, coordinates, direct neighbors, bond order and bond length. Selecting two atoms with the ruler gives their direct Euclidean distance; that measurement does not assert a chemical bond or interaction. Searching an atom by index/name provides keyboard access and a reproducible path to atom selection.

Sources are labeled as RDKit conformer, AlphaFold 3 prediction, or PDB experimental structure. RDKit conformational energy is never shown as binding affinity. pLDDT coloring is only offered when an AlphaFold scene actually contains confidence values; experimental B factors are displayed separately. RCSB's official [structure-quality guidance](https://www.rcsb.org/docs/general-help/assessing-the-quality-of-3d-structures) distinguishes prediction confidence, stereochemical quality and agreement with experimental data. Long/short bond geometry warnings remain visible in the viewport footer.

## Resource ownership and limitations

Declarative meshes, geometries and materials are disposed by [React Three Fiber on unmount](https://r3f.docs.pmnd.rs/api/objects); custom backbone geometries are disposed explicitly. Atom and cylinder instance buffers are updated together with their bounding spheres. Pixel ratio is capped at 1.75 and large atom sets use lower sphere tessellation.

This viewer currently targets WebGL 2 through the stable Three.js WebGL renderer. It does not advertise an untested WebGPU path. A renderer error has a visible fallback while the DOM atom inspector remains available. It does not infer electron density, molecular orbitals, binding affinity, secondary-structure assignments or atom-level confidence when that information is absent.

For a deterministic interaction check: open a conformer, search atom `0`, inspect its neighbor coordinates, enable the ruler, search `0` then `1`, and compare the displayed distance to the same JSON coordinate difference. Switch representations, toggle hydrogen visibility, focus/reset the camera, and inspect provenance. Open the real `5IKR` reference separately to check the protein trace and confirm pLDDT is unavailable for experimental B factors.
